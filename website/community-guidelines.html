Community Guidelines

Play fair, post fair.

Last updated 1 September 2026

Smashio's feed and posts (docs/social-plan.md) put what you write in front of other players, not just the people in your game chat. These guidelines are what we expect from every post, comment and profile — on top of, not instead of, the Terms of Service.

Not tolerated, ever

  • Harassment, hate speech, or threats directed at another player.
  • Sexual content, or content involving a minor in any context.
  • Content that promotes violence, self-harm, or illegal activity.
  • Doxxing — sharing someone else's phone number, address, or other private details without consent.
  • Impersonating another player, club, or venue.

We have a zero-tolerance policy for the above. A single confirmed instance can mean immediate content removal and account suspension — no warning required.

Also not welcome

  • Spam, unrelated advertising, or repeatedly posting the same thing.
  • Fake "looking for players" posts for games that don't exist.
  • Coordinated pile-ons or brigading another player's posts or profile.

How it's enforced

Every post and comment is checked automatically before it's published. Anyone can report a post, comment, photo, club, or profile directly from the app — reports go to a human queue with a 24-hour response target. You can also block another player at any time; blocking is immediate and mutual, no report needed.

Report it

See something that shouldn't be here? Use the report button on the post or profile in question, or email hello@smashio.com.au if you can't access the app.
