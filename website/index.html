How it works

Court to court in three taps

01

Find a game near you

Live listings ranked by distance and start time. Filter by level, or just tap Tonight and see what's on.

02

Join it, or host your own

Hold to request a spot and the host approves. Hosting takes under a minute. Court cost splits evenly, automatically.

03

Show up and play

Directions, group chat and a countdown in your pocket. Turn up, play, rate the session. Your reliability does the rest.
